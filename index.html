The Harsh Reality of Vaping

Vaping is often presented with flashy packaging and sweet flavors, but behind the marketing lies serious physical and mental health risks.

Up to 99%

of e-cigarettes sold in assessed venues contain high levels of addictive nicotine—even some labeled 0% nicotine.

🧪 Chemical Cocktail

E-cigarette aerosols aren't plain water vapor. They contain diacetyl (a chemical linked to severe lung disease), heavy metals like nickel and tin, cancer-causing chemicals, and volatile organic compounds.

🫁 Heavy Lung Impact

Inhaling vape chemicals causes airway inflammation and can lead to irreversible conditions like Popcorn Lung or acute conditions like EVALI (E-cigarette or Vaping Use-Associated Lung Injury).

🧠 Brain Rewiring & Anxiety

Nicotine impacts brain development in teenagers, affecting focus, learning, and self-control. It also creates a cycle of withdrawal stress that can make anxiety feel worse over time.

💸 Financial Drain

A pod or disposable vape habit can easily cost upwards of $1,500 to $2,000 a year—money that could be spent on trips, hobbies, tech, or savings instead.

🎯 Target Marketing

Vape manufacturers intentionally design products with sweet candy flavors, sleek USB-like shapes, and social media ads to target teens and build lifelong customers.

🌿 Better Alternatives

Staying vape-free gives you clear lungs, peak athletic performance, more control over your habits, and freedom from nicotine reliance.
